Evidence vocabulary

How to read the interface

These labels describe evidence or representation status. They are not interchangeable. Thread 20 also records the admitted visual classes in docs/visual_provenance_matrix_self_similar_cy_visualizer_v0_21.json.

Lean formalized
A definition or theorem is present in the sealed Lean core.
Runtime metadata
A value is validated or derived by the JavaScript runtime; theorem status does not follow from that.
Structural
The runtime represents relations, depths, or organization without realizing the underlying geometry.
Symbolic
An expression or annotation is retained symbolically instead of being expanded into geometric objects.
Unresolved
The current repository intentionally has no concrete representation or established result for this item.
Not materialized
A possible structural referent is not instantiated as sheets, fibers, covering geometry, or other concrete objects.
Engineering
An implementation, representation-safety, CI, performance, or viewport statement rather than a mathematical theorem.

Formal, runtime, camera, and engineering boundaries

A green formal-lean job means the scoped Lean modules build and pass their direct checks. A green runtime-contracts job means the JavaScript contracts and claim-discipline checks pass. Neither statement means that every mathematical idea named by the project has been formalized.

“Infinite navigation” means arbitrarily continued finite structural navigation. Structural camera scale is an SVG viewport parameter, distinct from scene.derived.metricScale. JavaScript safe-integer checks and benchmark horizons are engineering constraints or observations, not mathematical bounds.
