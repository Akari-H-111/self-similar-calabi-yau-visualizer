Interactive structure

Structural diagram · Interactive pullback tower

The diagram is structural, not a geometric realization. Expand materializes structural descriptors through the sealed recursion API; select, refocus, and collapse are navigation/presentation operations. Camera navigation reads that state only as a target and does not modify it. For each visible positive-depth transition, an aggregate D⁴ badge communicates the exact organization multiplicity without materializing D⁴ graphical objects. Arithmetic overlay controls only toggle source-backed symbolic annotations attached to this same structural canvas. The visible DOM/SVG is a bounded active render window and may omit presentation-visible levels that remain retained by the semantic model.

Keyboard users can use the camera buttons directly. Plain wheel/trackpad scrolling and touch pinch remain reserved for page/browser behavior; hold Alt while wheeling over the structural diagram to zoom the presentation camera. Ctrl/Cmd + wheel is never intercepted by the structural camera.

Symbolic arithmetic overlays

Nodes denote finite structural level descriptors and arrows denote pullback relations already present in the runtime model. D⁴ badges denote exact aggregate structural organization multiplicity only. Arithmetic markers are symbolic annotations with explicit evidence/provenance, not geometric arithmetic loci. Selection, focus, collapse, arithmetic overlay toggles, and camera state remain separate presentation/runtime domains. Camera scale is presentation-only viewport state.

Evidence vocabulary

How to read the interface

These labels describe evidence or representation status. They are not interchangeable. Thread 20 also records the admitted visual classes in docs/visual_provenance_matrix_self_similar_cy_visualizer_v0_21.json.

Lean formalized
A definition or theorem is present in the sealed Lean core.
Runtime metadata
A value is validated or derived by the JavaScript runtime; theorem status does not follow from that.
Structural
The runtime represents relations, depths, or organization without realizing the underlying geometry.
Symbolic
An expression or annotation is retained symbolically instead of being expanded into geometric objects.
Unresolved
The current repository intentionally has no concrete representation or established result for this item.
Not materialized
A possible structural referent is not instantiated as sheets, fibers, covering geometry, or other concrete objects.
Engineering
An implementation, representation-safety, CI, performance, or viewport statement rather than a mathematical theorem.

Formal, runtime, camera, and engineering boundaries

A green formal-lean job means the scoped Lean modules build and pass their direct checks. A green runtime-contracts job means the JavaScript contracts and claim-discipline checks pass. Neither statement means that every mathematical idea named by the project has been formalized.

“Infinite navigation” means arbitrarily continued finite structural navigation. Structural camera scale is an SVG viewport parameter, distinct from scene.derived.metricScale. JavaScript safe-integer checks and benchmark horizons are engineering constraints or observations, not mathematical bounds.
