Evidence vocabulary

How to read the interface

These labels describe evidence or representation status. They are not interchangeable.

Lean formalized
A definition or theorem is present in the sealed Lean core.
Runtime metadata
A value is validated or derived by the JavaScript runtime; theorem status does not follow from that.
Structural
The runtime represents relations, depths, or organization without realizing the underlying geometry.
Symbolic
An expression or annotation is retained symbolically instead of being expanded into geometric objects.
Unresolved
The current repository intentionally has no concrete representation or established result for this item.
Not materialized
A possible structural referent is not instantiated as sheets, fibers, covering geometry, or other concrete objects.
Engineering
An implementation, representation-safety, CI, or performance statement rather than a mathematical theorem.

Scope and provenance

What is established here?

Lean formalized

  • Four-coordinate coordinate-power map.
  • Coordinate-power iteration law.
  • Abstract set-theoretic pullback recurrence and its coordinate-power closed form.

Runtime / structural

  • Validated parameters and finite depth state.
  • D² runtime metadata and D⁴ organization metadata.
  • Lazy level descriptors, structural focus, aggregate organization, symbolic overlays, and the SVG structural projection.

Unresolved / not materialized

  • W has no concrete representation in this visualizer.
  • No Calabi–Yau hypersurface geometry, genuine sheets, covering geometry, or metric realization is rendered here.
  • Cyclotomic, torsion, collision, and related parent-project overlays remain deferred unless exact canonical source evidence is available.
Formal, runtime, and engineering boundaries

A green formal-lean job means the scoped Lean modules build and pass their direct checks. A green runtime-contracts job means the JavaScript contracts and claim-discipline checks pass. Neither statement means that every mathematical idea named by the project has been formalized.

“Infinite navigation” means arbitrarily continued finite structural navigation. JavaScript safe-integer checks and benchmark horizons are engineering constraints or observations, not mathematical bounds.
